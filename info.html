About

Dylan Forsberg is a photographer, director, writer, and publisher.

His photographs have appeared in Vogue China, Dazed & Confused, and i-D. He has worked with Chanel, Zadig & Voltaire, and Lancôme, and his landscape photographs have been printed on T-shirts sold through Madewell and boutiques worldwide.

He founded Transmission Magazine in 2012, publishing more than ten issues and printing over 50,000 copies.
