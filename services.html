Process

Photography and video projects follow a straightforward three-step process.

  1. 01

    Plan

    One brief preproduction discussion to establish the creative brief, review scripts or talking points, and plan the intended deliverables.

  2. 02

    Produce

    One scheduled production session, typically 6–8 hours, with a maximum of 8 hours including setup and breakdown.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    Our team handles post-production and delivers the finished content digitally on an agreed date.

The process is designed to require minimal involvement from the client.

Additional Expenses

Starting rates assume a client-provided creative brief.

Location and studio rentals, permits, travel, talent, styling, specialized equipment, licensing fees, and other production expenses are additional when required.

All additional expenses are quoted and approved in advance.

Monthly Partnerships

Monthly partnerships begin with a three-month commitment and automatically renew for successive three-month terms unless canceled at least 30 days before the end of the current term.

Founding Client Rates are guaranteed for the initial contract term. Any proposed pricing changes for subsequent terms will be communicated in advance, with the opportunity to decline renewal.

Services are billed monthly in advance. Standalone projects are available without an ongoing commitment.

All projects are subject to an agreed scope of work and service agreement.
