Process

Photography and video projects follow a straightforward three-step process.

  1. 01

    Plan

    Establish the brief, review scripts or talking points, and plan deliverables in one preproduction discussion.

  2. 02

    Produce

    One scheduled session, typically 6–8 hours, including setup and breakdown. Maximum: 8 hours.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    Our team handles post-production and delivers the finished content digitally on the agreed date.
